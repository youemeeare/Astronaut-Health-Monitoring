# NASA data, evidence and project positioning — v1.0.0

## What this build actually uses

1. **NASA OSDR live study-metadata search:** `GET /api/v1/nasa/search` queries the public OSDR search endpoint. It returns study metadata/search results. It does not automatically download raw assay files or convert them into astronaut telemetry.
2. **NASA Human Research Program hazard/risk resources:** used as the conceptual framework for the five hazard domains and associated risk areas. These are reference materials, not a machine-readable live data feed.
3. **NASA OSDR datasets uploaded by the team:** users may manually import CSV/JSON records with `NASA_RESEARCH` provenance. They are stored as raw research rows in `ResearchDatasetRow`, separate from `Reading` telemetry. The team must retain accession, cohort, assay, unit, source, access status and citation in the accompanying research notes. The hazard-observation API requires an evidence/accession reference when the source is labelled `NASA_OSDR` or `CSA_RESEARCH`.
4. **Synthetic prototype data:** generated by the demo scenario endpoint and labelled `SIMULATED`. It is not NASA data and not astronaut telemetry.
5. **Manual/sensor data:** user-entered or locally imported records labelled `MANUAL` or `SENSOR`; no NASA hardware feed is connected by default.

**No specific raw NASA study accession is bundled or currently used to train/validate the application.** The OSDR search connection is real; a dataset search result is not the same as downloading, validating, analysing or integrating that dataset.

## Candidate NASA studies for a future, properly documented analysis

These are research candidates, not data already integrated into v1.0.0:

- **OSD-575 — SpaceX Inspiration4 Blood Serum Metabolic Panel and Immune/Cardiac Cytokine Arrays.** Potentially relevant to immune/cardiovascular research. It is a short, 3-day commercial mission cohort, so it must not be treated as a proxy for a multi-year Mars mission. Inspect the files, variables, units, timepoints and access conditions before analysis.
- **OSD-573 — SpaceX Inspiration4 Dragon Capsule Metagenomic and Metatranscriptomic Microbial Swabs.** Potentially relevant to closed-habitat microbial research. It describes capsule surface swabs and microbiota sequencing, not a direct measurement of astronaut infection or clinical risk.
- **OSD-572 — SpaceX Inspiration4 oral, nasal and skin microbial swabs.** Potentially relevant to crew microbiome research; do not infer disease from sequencing results without validated methods and domain expertise.
- **Spaceflight Standard Measures research** describes measures spanning sleep/wake cycles, cognition, immune function, blood/urine chemistry, microbiome, cardiovascular function, sensorimotor function and team processes. Access to identifiable crew data is governed; use only authorized, appropriately de-identified/public data.

Always verify current file-level access conditions, license, data dictionary, units, population, study design, timepoints and citation at the source. Some human-derived sequence or other sensitive files require a controlled-access request and approval.

## Key capabilities

- **Hazard evidence ledger:** stores typed, source-labelled observations for radiation, behavioral, distance, gravity, habitat, immune, cardiovascular and sensorimotor domains. It validates a small metric vocabulary and keeps evidence references/context. It deliberately does not convert a raw observation into a medical risk score.
- **Deep-space communication scenario:** calculates one-way and round-trip delay (up to the user-configured 20-minute one-way scenario), outage duration and queued payload size. It is a deterministic tabletop calculator, not a network emulator, actual store-and-forward transport or communication service.
- **Research transparency panel:** explicitly distinguishes connected OSDR metadata search, manually imported research files, synthetic data and live operational telemetry (not connected).
- **Digital-twin no-data response:** reports a clear conflict response when no telemetry exists rather than implying a simulation can run without an observed state.

## What ASTRA-MED X is and is not

ASTRA-MED X is a student-built research demonstrator exploring a unified, provenance-aware interface across heterogeneous space-health evidence, structured crew self-report, transparent rule-based review and communication-delay workflow scenarios.

It does **not** replace NASA medical operations, flight surgeons, biomedical flight controllers, existing spacecraft medical systems, approved procedures, NASA OSDR, or NASA's Human Research Program. It does not diagnose, prescribe, control spacecraft systems, provide certified radiation dosimetry, or predict individual astronaut outcomes.

## Defensible project value

Do not claim NASA has no medical system or that NASA systems are deficient. NASA publicly describes medical operations, flight surgeons, biomedical flight controllers and downlinked physiological, dosimetry, exercise and environmental data. NASA also documents inherent mission constraints such as limited stowage, disrupted ground communications, radiation exposure and functional limitations in microgravity.

A defensible contribution for this prototype is narrower:

- Demonstrate a **student-built, interoperable evidence workspace** that keeps heterogeneous research rows separate from crew telemetry while preserving provenance.
- Demonstrate a **transparent hazard observation ledger** across the five NASA hazard categories and related research domains.
- Demonstrate **delay-aware workflow arithmetic and offline-first design requirements** for deep-space scenarios.
- Make data gaps and uncertainty visible instead of silently interpreting missing measurements as normal.
- Provide a reproducible sandbox for testing workflows using clearly labelled synthetic data.

These are prototype capabilities and design explorations, not proof of superiority over NASA's operational systems. Any claim of improved performance requires a defined comparison, approved datasets, metrics, and independent evaluation.
